Sports & Leagues

Quick guides to the sports Americans follow most.

Illustrations are original graphics. League facts are general and may change.

NFL Football

The NFL's 32 teams play a 17-game regular season that ends in the Super Bowl.

  • Competitions: NFL regular season, playoffs, Super Bowl
  • Teams and players: Historic franchises include the Green Bay Packers and Dallas Cowboys.
  • Key rule: Teams get four downs to gain 10 yards.
Related news

NBA Basketball

The NBA has 30 teams and an 82-game regular season.

  • Competitions: NBA regular season, Play-In, NBA Finals
  • Teams and players: Franchises such as the Boston Celtics and Los Angeles Lakers have long histories.
  • Key rule: Shot clock is 24 seconds; three-point line rewards range.
Related news

MLB Baseball

Major League Baseball's 30 clubs play a 162-game season.

  • Competitions: MLB regular season, postseason, World Series
  • Teams and players: Clubs like the New York Yankees and Chicago Cubs date back generations.
  • Key rule: Nine innings, three outs per half-inning, and a pitch clock.
Related news

NHL Hockey

The NHL has 32 teams and an 82-game season ending with the Stanley Cup Playoffs.

  • Competitions: NHL regular season, Stanley Cup Playoffs
  • Teams and players: Original Six teams include the Boston Bruins and Chicago Blackhawks.
  • Key rule: Three 20-minute periods, with overtime and shootouts in the regular season.
Related news

NCAA College Sports

College athletics span hundreds of schools, with football and basketball drawing the biggest US audiences.

  • Competitions: College Football Playoff, NCAA Tournament
  • Teams and players: Conferences like the SEC, Big Ten and ACC anchor the landscape.
  • Key rule: Athletes are student-athletes; eligibility rules apply.
Related news

Soccer

Soccer's US top flight is Major League Soccer, and the US co-hosted the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

  • Competitions: MLS, MLS Cup, U.S. Open Cup, NWSL
  • Teams and players: Many MLS clubs play in soccer-specific stadiums.
  • Key rule: Two 45-minute halves; offside rule applies.
Related news

Tennis

The US Open in New York is the American Grand Slam.

  • Competitions: US Open, ATP and WTA Tours
  • Teams and players: Players compete as singles or doubles.
  • Key rule: Points go 15, 30, 40, game; sets and tiebreaks decide matches.
Related news

Motorsports

American racing includes oval and road-course series.

  • Competitions: NASCAR Cup Series, IndyCar, Indianapolis 500, Formula 1 US Grand Prix
  • Teams and players: Teams are built around drivers, crews and engineers.
  • Key rule: Pit stops, flags and stage points shape race strategy.
