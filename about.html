An independent US sports information site.

About Sportify

Sportify publishes explainers, previews and reference information for American sports fans. We do not organize matches, host games, offer betting or sell tickets.

Our mission

Make US sports easier to follow with clear, accurate and accessible information.

What we cover

NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football and basketball, soccer, tennis and motorsports.

Editorial standards

We separate demonstration or sample content from verified reporting, label it clearly, and correct errors promptly.

How information is collected

Information comes from official league and team sources and licensed data providers where connected. Until then, data is labeled as demonstration data.

Contact

Email: configure SITE.email in assets/js/main.js. No postal address or phone number is published.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sportify live?

No. Scores and match data shown are demonstration data until a licensed data source is connected.

Do you offer betting or ticket sales?

No. Sportify is information only.

Are you affiliated with any league?

No. Sportify is independent and not affiliated with the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, NCAA or any team.
